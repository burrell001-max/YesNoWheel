NFL Picker Wheel

        Thirty-two teams, nicknames only

        The wheel carries all 32 NFL teams, one per slice, in city order from Arizona to Washington but labelled with the nickname alone. That is why the list starts with the Cardinals and the Falcons rather than the Bears: the slices follow the cities, not the alphabet of nicknames. Nicknames keep each label short enough to read on a phone, and the teams that share a metro area stay clearly separate.

        Nothing else is on the slices: no logos, no records, no odds and no divisions. Every team has the same 1-in-32 chance, and the wheel does not favour a team because it won last week or because it is popular.

        Running a draw with it

        Spin with the button, the wheel itself, or the space bar. The winner is marked by the pointer and named in a pop-up.

        • Elimination mode takes a team out once it has been drawn. For a sweepstake that is the whole point: 32 draws, 32 different teams, nobody doubled up.
        • Accumulation mode keeps every team available and tallies the draws, which suits a season-long pick challenge where repeats are part of the fun.
        • The text view lets you cut the list to one division or one conference, or add a friend's team so it can be drawn against the rest.
        • Weights are there if your game is not meant to be even, for example giving a weaker team two slices in a handicap draw.

        Five ways to use an NFL draw

        Office sweepstake

        One spin per entrant in elimination mode, with the list visible on a shared screen. Everyone sees their team drawn, which is the only thing that makes a sweepstake feel fair.

        Weekly pick challenge

        Spin at the start of the week and follow that team's result. Keep the wheel in normal mode so the same team can come up again, and record the picks in the history list to settle arguments later.

        Fantasy draft order

        Use elimination mode for the first 12 draws and read them out as the draft order. It takes a minute and removes the annual argument about who picks first.

        Video game franchise

        Spin before you start a career mode so the team is not your usual one. A team you would never choose is the point of the exercise.

        Follow-a-team month

        Draw one team and watch its next four games. It is a low-commitment way to learn a roster, and the guide to picking a team to root for sets out what to do in that first month.

        Questions people ask

        Why do I see nicknames instead of city names?

        Nicknames are shorter, so the label still fits on a phone screen. The order still follows the city, which is why the Cardinals come before the Falcons.

        Can I draw from one conference only?

        Yes. Delete the 16 teams from the other conference in the text view and the wheel becomes a conference draw.

        Are team logos shown?

        No. The wheel uses team names only. YesNoWheel is not affiliated with the NFL or its clubs, and no league marks are used.

        Does elimination mode reset between visits?

        The drawn teams come back when you reset the wheel or restore the list, and your last list is kept in the browser for next time.

        Can I add college or fantasy team names?

        Yes. The text view accepts any names, so the same wheel can run a college draw or a league of your own.

        Is it free to use?

        Yes. No account, no sign-up, and no limit on the number of draws.
