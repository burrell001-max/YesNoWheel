Image Picker Wheel
Spin to pick a picture

        1. What Is the Image Picker Wheel?

        Add your own pictures to a custom wheel and let it choose one at random. Good for choosing artwork, a photo, a meal or a destination.

        2. How to Use It

        Press Spin, click the wheel or hit the space bar. The pointer shows the winning slice and a pop-up shows the result. Open the entries list, add an image to each entry, then press Spin. Give each entry a short label so the result is easy to read.

        3. Ways to Use It

        • Pick a photo to share
        • Choose artwork to draw
        • Select a meal from pictures
        • Classroom picture prompts
        • Decide between places

        Want a different kind of wheel? See all our tools or read tips on the blog.

        4. Frequently Asked Questions

        Are my pictures uploaded to a server?

        This site has no accounts. Your wheel is kept in your browser on your own device.

        Can I add a label as well as a picture?

        Yes. Each entry can have a name and an image.

        Is it free?

        Yes. It is free to use and needs no account.
