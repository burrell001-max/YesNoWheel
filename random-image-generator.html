Image Picker Wheel

        A wheel you fill yourself

        The picture wheel opens with six starter entries, none of them carrying a picture. Replace them with your own options: one picture per entry, with a short name if you want one. That makes it a different tool from the other wheels on the site, which arrive with their lists already loaded.

        An entry can be a photo from your device, or an address that points at a picture online. Either way the picture is drawn onto the wheel itself, so the slices show the actual options rather than a label for them.

        How to add pictures and spin

        Open the entries panel and add an entry for each option. Choose a picture for it, then give it a name if the result pop-up needs to be readable at a glance. When the list is ready, spin with the button, the space bar, or the hub at the centre of the wheel.

        • Keep it to six or eight entries. A picture wheel with twenty photos produces slices too small to tell apart.
        • Use the same aspect for every picture. Mixed portrait and landscape photos draw unevenly and the wheel looks untidy.
        • Elimination mode offers Hide choice on the winning picture, which is how you work through a set of choices one at a time.
        • The text view still works for the names, so you can paste a list of labels and add pictures afterwards.
        • Mystery wheel hides the pictures until the spin ends, if the point is a surprise rather than a comparison.

        Five uses for a picture draw

        Choosing artwork to draw

        Add six reference photos of subjects you would be happy to draw, then spin. It removes the ten minutes of scrolling that usually happens before anyone picks up a pencil.

        Dinner from photos

        Photograph five meals you can actually cook this week, add them, and spin. Seeing the dishes makes the choice faster than reading a list of names.

        Classroom picture prompts

        Add a set of images for a writing task and let the wheel choose the prompt. Every student sees the same picture chosen in the same way, which matters when the task is graded.

        Where to go

        Save four photos of places within a day's travel and spin. It is a decision between things you already want to do, which is the kind of choice a wheel is good at.

        Photo to share

        Load a shortlist from a trip and let the wheel pick the one to post, rather than opening the camera roll and closing it again.

        Where the pictures live

        They stay in your browser on this device. There is no account and no upload step, so a photo you add is not sent to a server to be stored. That also means the wheel is tied to the browser you built it in: clearing site data removes it, and a different device starts empty.

        Because the pictures are stored locally, a very large set of high-resolution photos can slow the page down. Resize before adding if you plan to keep a lot of them.

        Questions people ask

        Are my pictures uploaded to a server?

        No. They are stored in your browser on your device. There is no account and no upload step on this site.

        Can an entry be a picture with no name?

        Yes. An entry is kept as long as it has a picture or a name, so a wordless wheel of images works fine.

        How many pictures should go on one wheel?

        Six to eight is comfortable. Beyond that the slices get small and the pictures become hard to tell apart.

        What happens if I clear my browser data?

        The wheel is emptied, because the pictures were stored locally. Keep the originals on the device if you want to rebuild it.

        Can I use pictures I found online?

        Only if you have the right to use them. Images you add stay on your device, but the responsibility for the licence is yours.

        Does the wheel work on a phone?

        Yes. Adding a picture from the phone camera roll works the same way, and the wheel and result pop-up fit the screen.
