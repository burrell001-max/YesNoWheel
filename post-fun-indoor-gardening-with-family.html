How to Start Indoor Gardening With Your Family

Last updated: November 2, 2026

You do not need a yard. A sunny windowsill, a few cups and some seeds are enough to grow something together.

Why it works with kids

Plants change every day, so kids can see their effort pay off. Gardening also teaches patience and responsibility in a way a worksheet can't.

Fast growers to start with

Radishes and cress sprout in days. Beans grow quickly and climb a string. Herbs like basil and mint give you something to cook with. Sprouting a bean in a clear bag against a window lets kids watch the roots.

Set it up in 20 minutes

Use any container with drainage holes, a tray to catch water, and potting mix instead of garden soil. Put it where it gets about six hours of light. Water when the top of the soil feels dry.

Share the jobs

Let one person water, one measure growth with a ruler and one keep a plant journal. Rotate roles each week. Taking a photo every few days makes a fun time-lapse.

Try it on the wheel

Let the wheel decide whose turn it is to water.
