How to Solve the "What Should I Eat?" Problem

Last updated: October 2, 2026

You are hungry, nothing sounds good and every option feels wrong. Try this simple routine instead.

Why it is so hard

Too many choices wears you out. The more options you weigh, the less each one seems appealing.

Shrink the list

Choose a cuisine or type first, such as soup, noodles or a salad. Then list three meals you can make or order within twenty minutes.

Keep a go-to list

Write ten meals you enjoy and keep them in your phone. When you are stuck, you pick from your own list instead of starting from nothing.

Spin and go

Put those meals on a wheel. If the result disappoints you, that tells you what you wanted, so cook that instead.
