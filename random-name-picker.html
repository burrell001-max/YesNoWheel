Name Picker Wheel
Spin the wheel generator for names

        1. What Is the Random Name Picker Wheel?

        This is a free spin the wheel generator for names. Type or paste a list of names, spin, and the wheel picks one at random. It works for a classroom, a team meeting, a raffle, a giveaway or any time you need a fair way to choose a person. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to create.

        2. How to Use It

        Replace the example names with your own, one name per line. In the text view you can paste a whole list at once, for example from a spreadsheet or class roster, and you can also import a CSV, TXT or JSON file. Press Spin, click the wheel or hit the space bar, and the pointer shows the winner in a pop-up.

        Use Elimination mode to remove each winner after it is picked, so everyone gets exactly one turn. Give a name a higher weight to make it more likely, or change colors to make the wheel easier to read on a projector. Use the Share button to copy a link to your wheel.

        3. How Fair Is It?

        Each spin picks its winner using your browser's secure random number generator, then animates the wheel to land on that name. Every name has the same chance unless you change its weight. The speed of the spin and how you click do not affect the result, and earlier winners do not change later ones, except when Elimination mode is on and you have chosen to remove them.

        5. Tips for a Fair Draw

        • Check the list first. Remove duplicates and typos so no one is on the wheel twice.
        • Agree the rules before you spin. Decide whether winners are removed and whether the first result is final.
        • Spin where everyone can see. Showing the wheel on a shared screen or in fullscreen mode makes the result easy to trust.
        • Keep a record. The Results tab keeps a history that you can export as a CSV file.
        • Follow the rules for prizes. If you are running a prize draw or giveaway, check the local laws and platform rules that apply to promotions.

        6. Frequently Asked Questions

        How many names can I add?

        As many as you like. The wheel gets crowded past a few dozen names, so for a large class or event, run it in groups or use Elimination mode over several spins.

        Can I remove a name after it wins?

        Yes. Turn on Elimination mode and each winner is removed from the wheel after the spin, so no one is picked twice.

        Are my names saved or shared?

        Your list is kept in your browser on this device and is not sent to a server. If you use the Share button, the link contains your wheel, so only share it with people who should see the names.

        Can I paste names from a spreadsheet?

        Yes. Copy a column of names and paste it into the text view, one name per line, or import a CSV file.

        Can I use this as a spin the wheel generator for names?

        Yes. Paste or type your names, one per line, and spin. You can give a name extra weight, remove winners with Elimination mode, and share the wheel with a link.

        Is it free?

        Yes. It is free to use and needs no account.
