What to Cook for Guests When You Can't Decide

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Guests arrive in two hours and you have no plan. Narrow it down, then let a wheel make the final call.

Start with the people

Ask about allergies and dietary needs first. Then think about how much time you want to spend in the kitchen versus with your friends.

Dishes that cooperate

Pick food that can be made ahead: a big pot of chili, baked pasta, a roast that rests while you visit, or tacos with toppings guests assemble themselves.

Build a short list

Write down four or five dishes you have cooked before and know you can pull off. Skip the untested recipe for a night that matters.

Let the wheel pick

Add your list to the custom wheel, spin once, and accept the answer. You get your decision back in ten seconds and your evening back.
