Types of Keto Diet and How to Get Started Safely

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Keto is a very low-carb, high-fat way of eating. Here are the main versions and what to consider before you begin.

The four common types

Standard keto is the classic low-carb, moderate-protein, high-fat plan. Cyclical keto adds higher-carb days. Targeted keto allows carbs around workouts. High-protein keto raises protein and lowers fat a little.

Getting started

Cut back on bread, pasta, rice, sugar and most fruit. Build meals around eggs, fish, poultry, leafy vegetables, nuts, olive oil and avocado. Drink plenty of water and include enough salt, because early on many people feel tired, a pattern often called the keto flu.

Who should be careful

Keto is not right for everyone. People who take diabetes or blood pressure medication, have kidney or liver conditions, or are pregnant or breastfeeding should speak to a doctor first.

Think long term

Ask yourself whether you could eat this way in a year. A plan you can keep up beats a strict one you abandon in a month.
