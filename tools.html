Tools

Choose a free spin the wheel generator for names, numbers, food, colors or your own custom list. Every tool opens in the same wheel, and you can edit the entries before you spin.

The tools are grouped by how much of the list is already written for you. Team, state, country, letter, number, color and date wheels arrive with a fixed set: every NFL club, all fifty states, the alphabet, one to twenty. Those pages are shortcuts, and each one can still be edited, reweighted or cleared. The name, food and truth or dare wheels sit in the middle: a useful starter list that most people adjust before the first spin. The picker wheel is the general-purpose end of the shelf, with no list of its own, built for entries that exist only in your head or your spreadsheet.

Pick a topic wheel when the entries are a known set and you want the result in one click. Pick the custom wheel when the options are yours, when the list mixes kinds of things, when some entries need a picture, or when the odds should not be equal. If you are unsure, start with the topic wheel closest to the job and edit it; switching to a blank wheel is only worth it once the list stops fitting.

Some of these decisions are worth thinking about before you spin, and the blog covers them: building a shortlist that produces a usable answer, running a raffle the room can trust, and splitting chores without an argument.
