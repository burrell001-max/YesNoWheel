This page explains how YesNoWheel uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Local storage is a similar browser feature that lets a site remember information on your device without sending it back with every request.

What the site uses

  • Essential storage (always on): your wheel entries, pictures, settings, language, theme and your cookie choice are saved in local storage so they are there next time. This stays on your device and needs no consent.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, loaded through the standard gtag.js snippet, counts visits and shows which pages are used. It sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* unless consent denies them, which is the default in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland until Google's message is answered.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense and its partners use cookies to serve, limit and measure ads, and to personalise them where consent allows. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland that consent comes from Google's message; elsewhere ads are served by default and personalisation can be turned off at adssettings.google.com.

How consent works

The message you see comes from Google, not from this site. It is configured in our AdSense account and delivered by the AdSense tag: a European regulations message covering the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and a US state regulations message where those laws apply. There is no first-party consent banner to dismiss.

Consent Mode v2 defaults are written into every page before Google's script runs: denied for ad storage, ad user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, granted outside those regions. Answering Google's message updates those signals. Clearing this site's cookies and reloading brings the message back if you want to change your answer.

Managing cookies yourself

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them may stop ads or analytics from working, and clearing local storage will remove your saved wheels and settings. To manage personalised ads from Google, visit adssettings.google.com. To opt out of many third-party ad vendors, visit aboutads.info. How Google uses data from partner sites is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
