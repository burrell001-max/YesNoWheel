Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This page explains how YesNoWheel uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Local storage is a similar browser feature that lets a site remember information on your device.

What we use

  • Essential storage: the site saves your wheel entries, settings, language and theme in your browser so they are there next time. This stays on your device.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics, loaded directly and through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see which pages are used. It sets cookies such as _ga only if you accept. If you reject, Google Consent Mode limits measurement to cookieless signals and no analytics cookies are stored.
  • Advertising: if and when we show ads, Google AdSense and its partners will use cookies to show and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalise them. Advertising cookies are only used if you accept.

Managing cookies

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may stop ads or analytics from working, and clearing local storage will remove your saved wheels and settings. To manage personalised ads from Google, visit adssettings.google.com. To opt out of many third-party ad vendors, visit aboutads.info.

More information
