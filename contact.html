Email

This is the best way to reach me for feedback, bug reports, questions about the site, or privacy requests.

What to include

  • For a bug: your device, your browser and what you did before it happened.
  • For an idea: what you were trying to do and what would have made it easier.
  • For a privacy request: say what you would like me to do and which page or feature it concerns.

Replies

I reply as soon as I can. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal details by email.
