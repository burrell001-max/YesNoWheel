Feedback and Contact

Last updated: November 26, 2026

I read every message. Tell me what works, what does not, and what you would like to see next.

Email

YesNoWheel@outlook.com

This is the best way to reach me for feedback, bug reports, questions about the site, corrections to an article, or a privacy request. There is no contact form, so nothing you write goes through the site itself.

What to include

  • For a bug: your device, your browser, and what you did in the moments before it happened.
  • For an idea: what you were trying to do, and what would have made it easier.
  • For a correction: the page address and the sentence you think is wrong.
  • For a privacy request: what you would like me to do, and which page or feature it concerns.

If a saved wheel has disappeared

Wheels live in your browser rather than on an account, so a saved list can vanish when site data is cleared, when a private window closes, or when you move to another device or browser. That is expected rather than a bug. If you think a list was lost for some other reason, email the page address and the steps you took, and I will look into it.

When to expect a reply

I aim to reply within two working days. A busy week can take longer, and a message that arrives with the details above is much faster to answer than one that needs a follow-up question.

Privacy

Email sent to this address is used only to reply to you. It is not added to a mailing list and is not shared. How the site itself handles data is set out in the Privacy Policy, and the cookies it uses are listed in the Cookie Policy.

Please do not send passwords, card details or other sensitive personal information by email.
