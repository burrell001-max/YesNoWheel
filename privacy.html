Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information YesNoWheel (the "site") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The site is operated by Kent B. Williams.

Information you give us

You do not need an account to use the wheel. The names, options, settings and spin history you create stay in your own browser, in local storage on your device. We do not receive them. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the site and its hosting provider may collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and the time of your visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs under its own privacy statement. We use Google Tag Manager and related analytics tools to understand how many people visit and which pages are useful.

Cookies and similar technologies

The site and our partners use cookies and local storage to remember your settings, measure traffic and show ads. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to control them.

Advertising

We use or may use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com, or opt out of many third-party ad vendors at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may link to other websites. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

How we use information

We use information to run and improve the site, measure traffic, keep it secure, show ads that keep it free, and answer your messages. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and rights

You can clear the wheel data stored by the site in your browser settings, block cookies, or use your browser's privacy controls. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email YesNoWheel@outlook.com.

Children

The site is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: YesNoWheel@outlook.com.
