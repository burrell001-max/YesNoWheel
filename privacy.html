Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what YesNoWheel (the "site") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The site is operated by Kent B. Williams.

What stays on your device

You do not need an account to use anything here. The names, options, pictures, colours, titles, language and theme you set stay in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us, and clearing site data removes them. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply.

Information collected automatically

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs such as IP address, browser type and requested pages under its own privacy statement. We also use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are used. Analytics cookies are set only if you accept them in the consent banner; if you reject, Google Consent Mode keeps measurement to cookieless signals and no analytics cookie is stored. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising

The site shows advertising through Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-5783852883880667). Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Personalised advertising is used only where you have consented. You can review or change your ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com, opt out of many third-party ad vendors at aboutads.info, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies and consent

On your first visit a banner asks you to accept or reject non-essential cookies, and the defaults are set to denied until you choose. Accepting allows analytics and advertising cookies; rejecting keeps them off. The Cookie settings link in the footer of every page reopens the banner so you can change your mind, and your choice is stored locally on your device. The Cookie Policy lists what each category does.

How information is used

Information is used to run and improve the site, measure which pages are useful, keep the site secure, show the ads that keep it free, and answer your messages. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and rights

You can clear the data this site stores in your browser settings, block cookies, use your browser's privacy controls, or change your consent choice with the footer link. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email YesNoWheel@outlook.com.

Children

The site is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed, and material changes will be described here rather than made silently.

Contact

Questions about this policy: YesNoWheel@outlook.com.
