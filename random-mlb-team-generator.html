MLB Picker Wheel

        Thirty clubs, nicknames on the slices

        All 30 Major League clubs are on the wheel, one slice each, in city order from Arizona to Washington and labelled with the nickname alone. Three cities field two clubs, so the nicknames are doing real work here: Cubs and White Sox, Dodgers and Angels, Yankees and Mets. Without them, half the slices would read the same.

        Every slice is identical in size, so each club has a 1-in-30 chance. Nothing on the wheel mentions a league, a division, a record or a payroll, because a draw for a team should not quietly favour the ones having a good season.

        Using it for a real draw

        Spin with the button, a click on the wheel, or the space bar. The pointer marks the winner and a pop-up names the club.

        • Elimination mode removes a club after it wins. For a draft order that is exactly what you want: the first ten draws are ten different clubs in the order they landed.
        • Accumulation mode keeps all 30 available and tallies the results, which suits a long season of small bets where repeats are expected.
        • The text view is where you narrow things down: one league, one division, or the six clubs you would actually watch.
        • Weights let a game give a rival two slices, so a friendly bet is not a straight coin toss.

        Five ways to use an MLB draw

        Season trial

        Draw a club, then follow its results for a month before deciding anything. A month is long enough to learn a few names and short enough to abandon without guilt.

        Fantasy draft order

        Run elimination mode and read the first ten or twelve draws as the draft order. It takes a minute, everyone watches it happen, and nobody has to argue about who picked the order.

        Ballpark shortlist

        Draw a club and put its park on the list of grounds you want to visit. Draw again next month and the list builds itself without a spreadsheet.

        Scoreboard game

        Spin for two clubs, then follow both for a week and compare series results. It is a light way to care about games you would otherwise skip.

        Video game career

        Spin before starting a franchise so you manage a club you would never have chosen. The draw is the point: a familiar team makes for a familiar season.

        Questions people ask

        Why are the slices nicknames and not full club names?

        Nicknames are shorter, so the text still fits on a phone screen. The order follows the city, which is why the Diamondbacks come before the Braves.

        Can I spin within one league or division?

        Yes. The wheel does not label leagues, so delete the clubs you do not want in the text view. Both leagues have fifteen clubs and each division has five.

        Are club logos or uniforms shown?

        No. The wheel shows names only. YesNoWheel is not affiliated with Major League Baseball or its clubs.

        How do I set a draft order?

        Switch to Elimination mode and spin once per pick. Each club is removed as it is drawn, so the order cannot repeat a team.

        Can I add minor league or fantasy teams?

        Yes. The text view takes any names, so the same wheel can run a league of your own.

        Is the wheel free?

        Yes, with no account and no limit on spins.
