        1. What Is the Yes or No Wheel?

        It is a wheel with Yes and No slices. Spin it and it lands on one, giving you a random answer. Nothing to install, no account to make. Open the page and spin.

        Switch to Yes, No or Maybe when "not yet" is an acceptable answer too.

        2. How to Use the Wheel

        Think of a question with a yes or no answer. Press Spin, click the wheel, or hit the space bar. The pointer shows the winning slice and a pop-up shows the answer.

        The running scoreboard counts every result, and the Results tab keeps a history of your spins.

        3. Yes / No vs. Yes / No / Maybe

        Yes / No gives a firm answer. Yes / No / Maybe adds a middle result, which suits choices where waiting is fair, like "Should I decide tomorrow?" You can also spin up to five sets at once to see a tally.

        4. Build Your Own Wheel

        Open Custom Wheel to enter your own names or options. Give each entry a weight, a color or an image, then spin to pick one. Use Elimination to remove each winner, or Accumulation to keep a leaderboard.

        You can import a list from CSV, TXT or JSON, and share your wheel with a link or embed it on another page.

        5. When to Use It

        The wheel works best for small choices where both answers are fine:

        • Eat out tonight?
        • Go to the gym?
        • One more episode?
        • Try something new?
        • Start the game?

        Classrooms and teams

        Decide whether to run another round, give a group a second chance, or add a bonus challenge.

        Party games

        Spin to decide whether a player takes the dare, answers the question, or passes the turn.

        6. Get a Better Answer

        Ask one clear question

        "Should I cook dinner tonight?" works better than "What should I do tonight?" Both Yes and No need to be real answers.

        Set your rule first

        Decide before you spin: take the first result, or go best of three. This stops you from spinning until you hear what you wanted.

        Notice your reaction

        If the wheel says No and you feel disappointed, you already know what you wanted.

        Keep it low-stakes

        Do not use a random result for health, safety, money or legal decisions. Use reliable information and a professional for those.

        8. Frequently Asked Questions

        Is the Yes or No wheel random?

        Yes. Each spin picks its result at random, then the wheel animates to that slice.

        Can I spin more than once for the same question?

        You can spin as often as you like. For a fairer decision, choose your rule first, such as accepting the first spin or best of three.

        Should I use it for important decisions?

        No. It is meant for fun and low-stakes choices. Rely on good information and professional advice for serious matters.

        Does it work offline, and is my data private?

        Once the page has loaded it runs in your browser. Your lists, settings and history stay on your device.

        Does it cost anything?

        No. The wheel is free and needs no account.
