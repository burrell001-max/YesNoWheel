    1. What Is the Yes or No Wheel?

    It's a wheel with Yes and No slices. You spin it, it lands on one, and you get a random answer. Nothing to install and nothing to create. Open the page and spin.

    Switch to Yes / No / Maybe when "not yet" is an acceptable answer too.

    2. How to Use the Wheel

    Think of a question that has a yes or no answer. Press the Spin button in the middle of the wheel, or click the wheel itself. The pointer at the top shows the winning slice, and the answer appears under the wheel.

    The scoreboard on the left counts every result. Press Reset score to start from zero.

    3. Yes / No vs. Yes / No / Maybe

    Yes / No gives you a firm answer. Yes / No / Maybe adds a middle result, which suits choices where waiting is fair, like "Should I decide tomorrow?"

    4. Scores and Results

    Tap the trophy to see every answer from this visit, newest first. The wheel keeps the list only in your browser and clears it when you close the page.

    5. When to Use It

    The wheel works best for small choices where both answers are fine:

    • Eat out tonight?
    • Go to the gym?
    • One more episode?
    • Try something new?
    • Start the group game?

    Classrooms and teams

    Use it to decide whether to run another round, give a group a second chance, or add a bonus challenge.

    Party games

    Spin to decide whether a player takes the dare, answers the question, or passes the turn.

    6. Get a Better Answer

    Ask one clear question

    "Should I cook dinner tonight?" works better than "What should I do tonight?" Both Yes and No need to be real answers.

    Set your rule first

    Decide before you spin: take the first result, or go best of three. This stops you from spinning until you hear what you wanted.

    Notice your reaction

    If the wheel says No and you feel disappointed, you already know what you wanted.

    Keep it low-stakes

    Don't use a random result for health, safety, money or legal decisions. Use reliable information and a professional for those.

    7. Need More Than Yes or No?

    Try our other tools to build a wheel with your own names or options, or flip a coin if two outcomes are all you need.

    8. Tell Us What You Think

    Found a bug or have an idea? Send us a message and we'll look at it.

    9. Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the Yes or No wheel random?

    Yes. Each spin picks its result at random with your browser's secure random generator, then the wheel animates to that slice.

    Can I spin more than once for the same question?

    You can spin as often as you like. For a fairer decision, choose your rule first, such as accepting the first spin or best of three.

    What's the difference between Yes / No and Yes / No / Maybe?

    Yes / No gives a firm answer. Yes / No / Maybe adds a neutral result for choices that can wait.

    Should I use it for important decisions?

    No. It's meant for fun and low-stakes choices. Rely on good information and professional advice for serious matters.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. The wheel is free and doesn't need an account.
