What Is Holistic Health, and Why Does It Matter?

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Holistic health looks at the whole person, not just a symptom. Here is what that means in daily life.

The big idea

Holistic health treats physical, mental, emotional, social and sometimes spiritual wellbeing as connected. Poor sleep affects mood, and stress affects digestion, so it makes sense to look at the whole picture.

Areas to check in on

Move your body in ways you enjoy. Sleep on a steady schedule. Eat a mix of whole foods. Keep in touch with people you trust. Make time for rest and for things that give your day meaning.

Small habits beat big overhauls

Pick one area and make one change for two weeks, such as a ten-minute walk after lunch or a regular bedtime. When it feels easy, add another.

A caution

Holistic care works best alongside conventional medicine, not instead of it. If you have symptoms or a medical condition, talk to a qualified health professional before changing treatment.
