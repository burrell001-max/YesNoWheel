What Are the Four Parenting Styles?

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Psychologists describe four parenting styles. Knowing them helps you spot habits worth keeping and ones worth changing.

The four styles

Authoritarian parents set strict rules with little explanation. Authoritative parents set clear limits and also listen and explain. Permissive parents are warm but set few limits. Uninvolved parents offer little guidance or attention.

What the research suggests

Studies, starting with Diana Baumrind's work, link the authoritative style with better outcomes such as stronger self-esteem and school performance. That is a pattern across many families, not a rule for any one child.

Be firm and kind together

Explain the reason behind a rule. Offer choices within limits. Admit when you are wrong. Listen first when your child is upset, then solve the problem.

You are not a label

Most parents mix styles depending on the day. If you notice a habit you dislike, pick one small change to try this week.
