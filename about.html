About YesNoWheel

YesNoWheel is a free decision wheel for the small choices that eat up your day.

What it does

Spin the Yes or No wheel for a quick answer, switch to Yes, No or Maybe, or build your own wheel with names, weights, colors and images. You can remove each winner as you go, keep a running leaderboard, share a wheel with a link, or embed it on another page. It works in 10 languages and needs no account.

Who makes it

YesNoWheel is built and run by Kent B. Williams, an independent web developer. I make simple, fast tools and games that work on any device, and I keep them free by showing a small number of ads.

How we think about it

A wheel is for fun and low-stakes choices: dinner, game night, whose turn it is. It is not a replacement for good information or professional advice on serious matters like health, money or legal questions. Our blog has practical ideas for making everyday choices easier.
