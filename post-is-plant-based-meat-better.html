Is Plant-Based Meat Better for You and the Planet?

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Plant-based meat has a lighter footprint than beef, but the health picture depends on the product. Here is what to check.

What it is

Plant-based meat is made from ingredients like soy, peas and wheat, shaped and flavored to taste like burgers, sausages and nuggets.

The environment

Life-cycle studies generally find lower greenhouse gas emissions and land use than beef. The gap shrinks against chicken, beans and lentils.

Your health

Some products have less saturated fat than red meat, and others are high in sodium or heavily processed. Compare labels for protein, saturated fat and salt, and do not assume the plant-based label means healthier.

A flexible approach

You don't have to go all in. Swapping a few meals a week for beans, tofu or a plant-based burger is an easy start.
